# Business Performance Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Business Performance Intelligence Dashboard is a comprehensive Power BI solution designed to deliver real-time, actionable insights into sales, profitability, and operational performance.

It spans three focused pages which include:

- Executive Overview,
- Sales & Profit Analysis
- Management Analysis

The project consolidates critical KPIs including 183.09bn in total sales, 157.16bn in profit, an exceptional 85.84% profit margin, and 36.62% sales growth.

The dashboard highlights strong performance in the Retail segment, Computers and Storage categories, and high-growth brands such as Samsung, while identifying clear opportunities in underperforming areas including the Government segment, low-growth brands (Epson, HP), and products missing sales targets. Interactive visuals covering sales by category, product, region, channel, salesperson, and segment enable leadership to quickly identify top performers, address gaps, and drive data-informed decisions that maximize profitability and growth.

## Business Problem
Management needs a clear and interactive way to monitor business performance and identify areas that require attention.

The dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

1. What is the company's total revenue?
2. Which month generated the highest sales?
3. Which product generated the most revenue?
4. Which category contributes the most revenue?
5. Which category is the most profitable?
6. Which product has the highest profit margin?
7. Are there products generating high revenue but low profit?
8. Which customer type generates the most revenue?
9. Which region has the highest number of customers?
10. What payment method is most commonly used?
11. Who is the best-performing salesperson?
12. Which salesperson generates high sales but poor profit?
13. Which region is underperforming?
